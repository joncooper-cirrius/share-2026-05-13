The four objections, ground-truthed by 9 weeks of discovery

Objection 1

"I can build a CRM quickly and easily in Claude."

Joe (Salesforce): The visible 10% is a CRM interface; the 90% — enterprise security, multi-brand data architecture, integrations, ongoing maintenance — is the hidden engineering liability.

Cirrius — we measured the 90%

  • ~5 million records per brand entering the migration. PCMS records date back to 1980, with some carrying forced "1930" placeholders from the original paper-to-digital ingestion. — LG / PE discovery
  • 6 CRMs across the portfolio today. — Soo-Jin's own count, ETI Learning Session. Plus the auxiliary stack: i360, Centah, PCMS, Microsoft Dynamics 365 CE, Vendo, Folio, Hatch, HeavySet, RingCentral, Five9, BirdDog (~30 aggregator feeds), Sales Rabbit, Laserfiche, ADP, KPA, GeoTab, Workato, ~5 finance partners with per-lender APIs.
  • TCPA-noncompliant opt-in is baked into PCMS today. "Our system forcing an opt-in… is a challenge." — Melissa Skinner, PE. Direct regulatory exposure that a roll-your-own CRM would inherit.
  • "Millions of dollars" in lender dealer fees require per-lender API integration to manage. — Sven Kramer, PE. Five live finance partners at PE alone, each with unique app IDs.
  • 12 cross-brand foundation locks already negotiated through 8 sessions of scoping and 8 days of on-site discovery across LG, PE, Champion. — Lane 1 readbacks. That negotiation work doesn't come pre-built in any AI-generated CRM.

Objection 2

"Implementations of Salesforce are rarely smooth."

Joe (Salesforce): Phased delivery + firm SOW + Cirrius as a proven partner is exactly the structure that de-risks the rollout.

Cirrius — here is how this one is being de-risked

  • Hybrid waterfall + agile delivery model. Discovery across 6 brands → Global Operating Model designed once → focused implementation on LeafGuard (and likely Patio Enclosures). Avoids both the eleven-brand-waterfall risk and the brand-by-brand-agile-into-incompatible-builds risk.
  • 12 cross-brand foundation locks confirmed independently by three different operating models (LG, PE, CHW) before a line of code is written. When three brands converge on the same answer, it's a foundation lock, not a brand quirk.
  • Cirrius commitment on the timeline, from Jeff Loven (PM): "February 1st is a no-fail target — we can't mitigate the risk by adding buffer to the timeline." The team is managing holiday velocity loss without timeline slip.
  • Governance is settled in operator language, not Cirrius language. Brian Perry: "Post-sale RevOps is Grant. Pre that is Kristy. Soo-Jin resolves impasse. Brian is point of contact before C-suite."
  • The Nov-30-to-Feb-1 contract gap is already surfaced and addressed. Project-based SOW addendum for build / UAT / go-live, plus extending the pod for run-rate. No surprises in February.
  • Cirrius posture: "Most technical project setbacks are due to non-technical reasons." — Jeff Loven. Change management and decision velocity are the moat, not Salesforce expertise alone.

Objection 3

"Salesforce is just a master customer record."

Joe (Salesforce): Field Service, Marketing Cloud, Data Cloud, and Agentforce on top of the unified data layer turn Salesforce into the operating system for the business.

Cirrius — every Salesforce capability maps to a specific GDI operator ask

  • Sales Cloud + CPQ → Mike Moore's structured-discount-governance ask, the "Brian Perry's brain" pricing extraction, the $2.25B of stranded PE quote pipeline (PE wrote $2.5B and sold $250M in 2024).
  • Field Service + dedicated contractor portal → the PE subcontractor adoption pattern (DIRs, photos, COS signatures, ETA, material attestation) on personal devices, without forcing sub-as-employee.
  • Service Cloud + Entitlements → Champion's ~20 warranty variants "left to interpretation" (Kelly Meyers); PE's service AR that gets silently written off (Mike Moore); manufacturer problem-reports that today never make it back to the line.
  • Experience Cloud → cross-brand customer portal (LG's "pizza tracker" ask from David Rosser, PE's explicit ask from Mike Moore, foundation for cross-brand household orchestration).
  • Account hierarchies (house-centric model) → the federation thesis ratified by three brands independently. Brian Perry: "they are the home."
  • Workato + ERP-agnostic schema → bidirectional sync to Dynamics NAV today, holds through the 2027 enterprise-ERP consolidation. Job-cost, inventory, AR, per-lender APIs.

Objection 4

"At previous companies, Salesforce slowed us down instead of improving us."

Joe (Salesforce): That's the symptom of over-customization, under-investment in adoption, or a bloated org — not a flaw in the platform.

Cirrius — this rollout is structured to avoid each failure mode

  • LG is pulling for this, not pushing back. David Rosser and Amanda Opara have surfaced ask after ask through 8 sessions of scoping and 8 days of discovery — dummy-proofed paperwork, dispatch optimization, $1,200/hour crew-revenue tracking. The platform inherits an operator that sees modernizing as an imperative.
  • Global Design upfront is itself the anti-bloat structure. The over-customization trap happens when each brand bends the platform to its own legacy quirks. Twelve foundation locks negotiated upfront mean Phase 1 is being built against the design, not against eleven separate operating models.
  • Champion is the in-house proof. Already runs the house-centric customer model on Microsoft Dynamics 365 CE with auto-routing by skill and proficiency, 13,800 appointments / month, 70% sit rate, three API-funded customer-incentive engines. We are standardizing what one of the brands already proves works.
  • The KPI scoreboard is locked early. Five Charter Success Metrics, GDI-authored and leadership-validated: Set Rate, Speed to Lead, Consent Enforcement, Brand Visibility, Executive Reporting. Lead Conversion Rate explicitly removed (working-session decision: not what this Phase 1 can move).
  • Speed-to-Lead momentum is already in motion before the platform ships. Pedro Guimaraes: UWD went from high-30%s to high-70%s in eight weeks of operating discipline.
